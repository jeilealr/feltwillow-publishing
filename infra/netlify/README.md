# Netlify: spec-only hosting option

**Not configured.** The Free plan has a 300-credit monthly hard limit: production deploy 15 credits,
bandwidth 20 credits/GB, 2 credits per 10k requests. At exhaustion, **all projects of the team pause** until
the next cycle, and visitors see "Site not available".

Use only as a fallback after the owner records that this outage-instead-of-billing behaviour is accepted.
Commercial projects are allowed according to a Netlify blog statement; the terms of service have not been
reviewed.
